Research project · 2025 — ongoing

PESCA — EComInAS: Ecological Communities' Integral Assessment of Stability

Estación Biológica de Doñana, CSIC Funded by Junta de Andalucía (Emergia programme) Principal Investigator: V. Domínguez-García

EComInAS builds on the methods developed to study stability in plant-pollinator communities, extending them into a more general framework for assessing the stability of ecological communities. The project is funded through the Emergia programme of the Junta de Andalucía, and is based at the Estación Biológica de Doñana.
